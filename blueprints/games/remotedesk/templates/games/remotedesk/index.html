Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Glintdesk

How do I connect my phone to my PC?
Install Glintdesk on both devices and sign in with the same account. Your devices will automatically appear in each other's device list. Tap the PC you want to control to connect — no manual IP configuration needed.
Do I need to be on the same WiFi network?
No! Same-network connections are fastest (5-15ms latency), but Glintdesk also works across different networks. The app automatically selects the best connection method: LAN direct, P2P traversal, or server relay.
Is it free to use?
Yes. Core features are free to use with no time limits. We also offer premium features for power users who need advanced capabilities.
Is the connection secure?
Absolutely. All connections use AES-256 end-to-end encryption. Data is encrypted directly between your devices — our servers cannot decrypt or view any content. We also never store your session recordings.
What are the system requirements?
Mobile: Android 8 or later
Desktop: Windows 10 or 11
Network: Host PC should have at least 5 Mbps upload bandwidth for smooth streaming
Can I use it for gaming?
Yes! Glintdesk is optimized for gaming with latency as low as 5-15ms on 5GHz WiFi. It supports virtual gamepad controls and works with Xbox and PlayStation controllers connected to your phone. Great for strategy, RPG, simulation games, and even some action titles.
How is this different from TeamViewer or AnyDesk?
Glintdesk is purpose-built for mobile-to-PC control with deep optimization for touch interfaces and game streaming. We offer lower latency through intelligent P2P connections, a better mobile experience, and it's completely free without commercial use restrictions.
What if I need help?
Open the Glintdesk Android app, go to Me → Support, and submit a feedback form. We'll get back to you as soon as possible.
